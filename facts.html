💡 World Wonder

Open a fact card to learn something new.

The Pacific Ocean is the largest ocean.

It covers a huge part of Earth’s surface.

Mount Everest is the highest mountain above sea level.

It is in the Himalayas.

Russia spans 11 time zones.

That is one reason its local times can be very different from east to west.

The Sahara is the largest hot desert.

It stretches across much of North Africa.

Antarctica is the coldest continent.

It is also extremely dry and windy.

Greenland is the world’s largest island.

It is mostly covered by an ice sheet.

Lake Baikal is the deepest freshwater lake.

It is in Siberia, Russia.

The Andes are the longest continental mountain range.

They run along the western edge of South America.

The Panama Canal connects the Atlantic and Pacific oceans.

Ships can use it to avoid sailing around the southern tip of South America.

The Arctic Ocean is the smallest ocean.

It surrounds the North Pole.

Latitude measures north or south of the Equator.

The Equator is 0 degrees latitude.

Longitude measures east or west of the Prime Meridian.

The Prime Meridian is 0 degrees longitude.

Japan is an island nation with many volcanoes.

It lies along the Pacific Ring of Fire.

The Himalayas contain many of the world’s highest mountains.

Mount Everest is part of this mountain system.

The Gobi Desert is in Asia.

It stretches across parts of Mongolia and China.

The Atacama is one of the driest places on Earth.

It lies mainly in northern Chile.

Coral reefs support many marine species.

They provide food and shelter for ocean life.

Monsoons are seasonal changes in wind and rainfall.

They can bring wet and dry seasons.

Physical geography studies natural features and processes.

Examples include mountains, rivers, climate and rocks.

Human geography studies people and places.

It includes population, cities, culture and migration.

Ocean currents move heat around the planet.

They help influence climate.

Tides are strongly influenced by the Moon’s gravity.

The Sun also affects tides.

The Great Barrier Reef lies off Australia’s northeast coast.

It is one of Earth’s best-known coral reef systems.

A peninsula is land surrounded by water on three sides.

It is connected to a larger landmass.

A strait is a narrow waterway connecting larger bodies of water.

The Strait of Gibraltar connects the Atlantic and Mediterranean.
