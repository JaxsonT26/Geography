Explore the seven continents

🌍 Africa

54 countries. The Sahara, Nile River, savannas and tropical rainforests are major features.

🧊 Antarctica

No sovereign countries. It is the coldest, driest and windiest continent.

🌏 Asia

49 countries. It is the largest continent by land area and home to more than half of the world’s people.

🏰 Europe

44 countries. Europe contains many countries, languages and cultures in a relatively small area.

🌎 North America

23 countries. It includes Canada, the United States, Mexico, Central America and the Caribbean.

🌎 South America

12 countries. The Andes and the Amazon Basin are major geography features.

🌊 Oceania

14 countries. Australia, New Zealand and many Pacific island nations are part of Oceania.
