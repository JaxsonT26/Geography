🌎 The Seven Continents

🌍

Africa

54 countries

The Sahara, Nile River, savannas and tropical rainforests are major features.

🧊

Antarctica

No sovereign countries

The coldest, driest and windiest continent.

🌏

Asia

49 countries

The largest continent by land area and home to more than half of the world’s people.

🏰

Europe

44 countries

A continent of many countries, languages and cultures.

🌎

North America

23 countries

Includes Canada, the United States, Mexico, Central America and the Caribbean.

🌎

South America

12 countries

Home to the Andes and the Amazon Basin.

🌊

Oceania

14 countries

Includes Australia, New Zealand and many Pacific island nations.
