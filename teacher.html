5-MINUTE WARM-UP

Where in the world?

Display a country from the Country Explorer. Students write its continent, one nearby country, and one question they have about it.

DISCUSSION PROMPTS

Ask, don’t just tell.

Why do many people live near rivers?

Encourage students to think about fresh water, farming, transport, trade, and settlement.

How can a mountain affect people?

Discuss climate, transport, farming, tourism, and how mountains can separate regions.

What clues can a flag give us?

Look for colors, stars, crosses, plants, animals, symbols, or patterns connected to history and identity.

Does geography change?

Compare changing coastlines, rivers, cities, glaciers, and landforms with permanent geographic features.
